Privacy policy.

How Pro Choice Roofing collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Pro Choice Roofing (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it, in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Irish data protection law.

Who we are

Pro Choice Roofing, 20 Mount Eustace Crescent, Dublin 15, D15 RY6R, Ireland. Email prochoiceroofers@gmail.com, phone 087 438 5708.

What we collect

When you send a quote request, callback request or message, we collect the details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, the service you are interested in and your message. We do not collect any other personal information through this website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a roof inspection or quotation.
  • To carry out and follow up on work you have asked us to do.
  • To keep records required for accounting and legal purposes.

Our legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in replying to enquiries. We never sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Records relating to completed work are kept for as long as required by law.

Sharing

We only share your information with service providers that help us run our business (such as our email provider), and only as needed. We do not transfer it outside the EEA except where appropriate safeguards are in place.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, restrict or object to how we use it, or ask for a copy. Contact us using the details above. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
