About us

Reliable & experienced.

Pro Choice Roofing provides professional roofing services for homeowners and commercial clients throughout North Dublin and the surrounding areas. Our experienced team handles projects of every size, from minor roof repairs and storm damage to complete new roof installations.

We take the time to assess each roof properly, explain the available options and recommend the most practical solution for the property. Our focus is always on dependable workmanship, durable materials and clear communication from the initial inspection through to completion.

We specialise in new roof installations, roof repairs, flat roofing, chimney repairs, emergency roofing and guttering, fascia and soffit work. Every project is approached carefully, with close attention paid to weatherproofing, drainage, ventilation and the details that protect a property over the long term.

Whether you have discovered a leak, need an ageing roof replaced or want professional advice before starting a project, Pro Choice Roofing is ready to help. Contact us to arrange an assessment and receive a free written quotation.

Our services

Complete roofing, start to finish.

Pro Choice Roofing provides reliable roofing installations, repairs and maintenance across North Dublin and the surrounding areas.

New Roof Installations

Complete new roof installations for homes, extensions and commercial properties. We install slate, tile and flat roofing systems using durable materials designed to provide reliable, long-term protection in the Irish climate.

Learn more

Roof Repairs

Professional repairs for leaking roofs, broken or missing tiles, damaged flashing, loose ridge tiles and general wear. We identify the source of the problem and complete the necessary work to prevent further damage.

Learn more

Flat Roofing

Flat roof installation, repair and replacement for extensions, garages and commercial properties. We provide durable, weather-resistant systems with effective drainage and a clean, professional finish.

Learn more

Guttering, Fascia & Soffit

Installation and repair of guttering, fascia, soffits and downpipes. Our roofline solutions direct rainwater safely away from your property while protecting the roof structure and improving its appearance.

Learn more

Chimney Repairs

Chimney repointing, flashing repairs, capping, brickwork repairs and partial rebuilds. We resolve common chimney problems that can allow water to enter and damage the surrounding roof structure.

Learn more

Emergency Roof Repairs

Prompt assistance for roof leaks, storm damage, missing tiles and other urgent roofing problems. We assess the damage, secure the affected area and complete repairs to protect your property.
