FAQ

Roofing questions, answered.

The questions Dublin homeowners ask us most. Anything else, call us on 087 438 5708.

How much does a roof repair cost in Dublin?

Most small roof repairs in Dublin, such as replacing slipped tiles or re-sealing flashing, cost a few hundred euro. Larger repairs and replacements depend on the size and condition of the roof. We inspect for free and give you a clear written quote before any work starts.

Do you charge for a roof inspection or quote?

No. Roof inspections and written quotations are free, with no call-out fee and no obligation.

Do you do emergency roof repairs?

Yes. We are available 24/7 for leaks, storm damage and missing tiles across Dublin, Meath and Louth. We secure the roof first to stop further water damage, then complete the permanent repair.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Dublin, including Dublin 15, Blanchardstown, Castleknock, Swords, Malahide, Clontarf, Raheny and Lucan, plus Meath and Louth: Ashbourne, Ratoath, Dunboyne, Navan and Drogheda.

Are you insured?

Yes. Pro Choice Roofing is fully insured, and all workmanship is guaranteed.

How long does a new roof take?

A typical house re-roof in Dublin takes between three days and a week, depending on size, weather and the roofing system. We give you a timeline with your quote.
